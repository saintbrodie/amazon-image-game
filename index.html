About the game

What Did They Buy?

Guess products from customer review photos. The bundled demo uses original mock images. A local dataset builder can turn image-bearing Amazon Reviews 2023 records into game rounds without live-scraping Amazon.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon. Review images and metadata from third-party datasets may have separate rights and usage restrictions. Check those terms before publishing a dataset-backed deployment.
